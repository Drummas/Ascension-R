Mods that come with Ascension-R. None of them is on: to use one, copy its folder into the mods folder (the
launcher's Mods tab, Open folder). It shows in that tab, switched on, and the tab switches it off again.

Xbox button icons: A, B, X and Y in place of Cross, Circle, Square and Triangle, and View for Select.
Start, the shoulder buttons and the sticks stay PlayStation's.

Skip intro: skips the intro logo screens and notices at startup, going straight to the loading screen.
Replaces the boot screen list with an empty file; nothing from the game is bundled.
