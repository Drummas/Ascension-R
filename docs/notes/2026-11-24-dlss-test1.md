# DLSS test build 1: the first RTX reports, the HUD's jitter, and a hang at a cinematic (2026-11-24)

The `dlss-test-1` pre-release (dad5632) went to the RTX owners of issue #39. The first, on an RTX 5060 with a Ryzen 5
5600X, sent three reports (seven sessions): DLSS runs, and the three problems they saw are not DLSS's alone.

## DLSS on an RTX 5060

- `[dlss] NGX up, DLSS available`; `nvngx_dlss.dll 310.9.16, from the DLSS folder`; no evaluate failed and DLSS never
  fell back to FSR in any session.
- 1440p internal in a 1080p window (the swap chain at the render's size): DLAA at 2560x1440, 143 MB of VRAM. 720p in
  the 1080p window: Quality, made at 1280x720, 85 MB.
- The modes' ranges at 1440p: Quality, Balanced and Performance take 1280x720 to 2560x1440, DLAA 2534x1426 to
  2560x1440, Ultra Performance its 853x480 alone; at 1080p the same at 960x540 to 1920x1080. The choice by range and
  nearest scale picked what was expected.
- 40 to 50 frames a second in the first level with either, Unlock FPS on: the CPU sets the pace there.
- NGX's own lines were some 220 a session, all at its start (its model cache's misses among them): off by default
  now, `D3D12_DLSS_LOG=on` or `verbose` for them.

## The HUD shook, with DLSS and with FSR

At 720p in the 1080p window the reporter saw the HUD "very shaky" with DLSS Quality and with AMD FSR, and least with
DLAA. The upscaler's jitter went on every draw through the title's camera into a 16:9 target (`vp_record_cb`, as the
batch is recorded), and the title draws its HUD through the camera too (its 2D goes through it, as ultrawide found),
after a clear of the display's depth alone. The marker of that clear (`fg_marker`) is only known at the replay, too
late for the constants. So the HUD moved by the jitter, half a render pixel: three quarters of a display pixel at
720p in 1080p, three eighths at DLAA's 1440p shown at 1080p.

- The recording now sees the same clear (the display's depth alone, after a scene's draws as `WIDE_SCENE_MIN` counts
  them) and leaves the draws after it unjittered, until the flip.
- A frame whose last flip drew no scene (a menu: not upscaled) gets no jitter either.
- The log says it for the first frame and then for frames with more such draws than before, four times at most:
  `[D3D12] upscaler: the HUD's clear found as it was recorded; N camera draws after it, not jittered`. On this PC with
  FSR it was found in every session.

## A hang as the Megaera cinematic loaded

Two of the reporter's sessions, one with DLSS and one with FSR, both with Unlock FPS, stopped as `HECA200IGC.WAD` (the
cinematic after the prison's first parasites) loaded; two others went through it.

- Each time the VRAM guard said the pool was short (`[vram] the pool is short: 0x15560 bytes ... its top at 0x200 ...
  159.9 MB ... with 32.3 MB of holes between them; 0 moves queued`), five times, and the game then wrote to address 0
  (`[null-write]`, by func_0039B93C's caller func_0039BF48, in the loader func_003F863C) and stopped making frames:
  the FIFO parked on a JUMP to itself, the jump-park resync after 50 ms, and 15 s later the freeze dump with the SPU
  workloads idle. The FIFO is the aftermath, not the cause.
- The pool is a stack with holes the manager (func_003A0780) gives back by moves, a budget a frame. It does its pass
  only when the RSX has released label 0x4E with the frame counter (0x9B227C), and otherwise leaves the frame alone.
  32-38 MB of holes with no move queued looked like a manager that had left most frames alone, with Unlock FPS running
  the game ahead of the RSX. Counted on this PC it is not so: the fight's replay, FSR at 720p, GOWA_VRAM_MANAGER_LOG=1,
  one run each: at 60 frames a second the pass in 299-303 of each 600 frames; unlocked in 309-600, more often and with
  more frames a second. What runs the pool short there is not found yet.
- Not reproduced here yet: the reporter's saves on this PC's European disc, Unlock FPS, FSR at 720p: one play through
  the cinematic, no shortage. A replay of their recording did not keep to their route (the European disc's welcome page
  wants Circle, and a playback waiting at its first mark let no other press through; `PAD_REPLAY_WAIT_MS` and
  `PAD_ALWAYS_CHANGE` for such a splice).
- Counted now for the next reports, as the reporter's PC may differ: `gowa_vram_manager` (on the manager's entry) and
  `gowa_vram_manager_works` (where its pass begins, 0x003A0888), in windows of 600 frames. A shortage's line says the
  last window's; a window with the pass in fewer than a quarter is said once a minute; `GOWA_VRAM_MANAGER_LOG=1` says
  every window. ABI 10.
